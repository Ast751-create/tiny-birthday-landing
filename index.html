Frequently asked questions

What's included in the price?

Every package covers the cake, themed decor, food and play-area access. Larger packages add a photozone, piñata, dessert station and masterclass activities. Tap any package above to see the full list.

Is the cake tasting really free?

Yes — the tasting and terrace tour are completely free, with no deposit and no obligation. You only book once you're happy with the plan.

Can you do a specific theme or character?

Absolutely. From soft and elegant to bold themed parties, we style the celebration around your child's favourite theme.

Do you offer allergy-friendly cakes?

Yes — gluten-free and no-added-sugar cakes are available, and as a healthy cafe we're happy to accommodate common dietary needs.

How many guests can you host?

Up to 40 guests on the private tropical terrace. Activities are tailored to your children's ages.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend reserving 3–4 weeks ahead — weekend dates fill up fastest. Message us to check your date.
